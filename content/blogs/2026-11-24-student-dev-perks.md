> **TL;DR:** Most students don't realize their `.edu` or university email unlocks thousands of dollars in free IDEs, cloud credits, design tools, and ambassador programs. I put together an open-source list at [SeanNachapat/Student-Dev-Perks](https://github.com/SeanNachapat/Student-Dev-Perks) — here's a quick sample of what's inside.

When I started shipping side projects, setting up servers, and building hackathon prototypes in university, I kept running into student offers scattered across dozens of sites. Almost nobody tells freshmen how much production-grade software they can get for free just by having a school email.

So I collected them into one open-source repository: **[SeanNachapat/Student-Dev-Perks](https://github.com/SeanNachapat/Student-Dev-Perks)**.

If you only claim one thing first, start with the **[GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)** — verifying once there acts as a master key that unlocks dozens of partner perks automatically.

---

## A Taste of What's in the Repo

Here are just a few highlights from each section to give you an idea of what's available:

### Development Tools
- **[GitHub Pro & Student Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)** — GitHub Pro, Copilot, Codespaces credits, plus free 1-year domains (`.me`, `.tech`, `.live`).
- **[JetBrains All Products Pack](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/#students)** — Free access to IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, CLion, and more.
- **[MongoDB Atlas](https://www.mongodb.com/students) & [Sentry](https://sentry.io/welcome/github-students/)** — $50 database credits, free certifications, and 500k monthly error monitoring events.

### Cloud & Hosting
- **[Microsoft Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students/)** — $100 annual cloud credit + 25+ free services, no credit card needed.
- **[DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com/github-students)** — $200 in hosting credits valid for 1 year.
- **[Vercel](https://vercel.com/), [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) & [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/)** — Generous free tiers to deploy full-stack apps without spending a baht.

### Learning, Design & Productivity
- **[Frontend Masters](https://frontendmasters.com/welcome/github-student-developer-pack/) & [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/github-students)** — Months of free engineering, web dev, and AI courses.
- **[Figma Education](https://www.figma.com/education/) & [Autodesk](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/overview)** — Free Figma Professional (including Dev Mode) and 1-year CAD/3D licenses.
- **[Notion Education](https://www.notion.so/product/notion-for-education) & [Microsoft 365](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/education/products/office)** — Notion Plus, Office apps, and 1 TB of OneDrive storage.

### Student Ambassador & Campus Leader Programs
Beyond software, the repo also lists **10+ campus leadership programs**—including **GitHub Campus Experts**, **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors**, **Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC / GDG)**, and **AWS Student Builder Groups**—for students who want mentorship, event funding, certifications, and community experience.
